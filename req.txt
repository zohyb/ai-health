# AI-Based Health Diagnosis Portal

## Project Overview

The *AI-Based Health Diagnosis Portal* is a web-based intelligent healthcare decision-support system designed to assist users in performing preliminary health assessments using Artificial Intelligence (AI).

The system allows users to enter symptoms in natural language and optionally upload medical images (such as skin conditions, swelling, redness, allergies, bruises, or visible infections). The AI engine analyzes both textual symptoms and uploaded images to predict possible diseases, assess risk levels, generate confidence scores, and provide personalized health recommendations.

The system follows a *multi-agent AI architecture*, where different AI agents collaboratively perform specific tasks such as symptom extraction, image analysis, disease prediction, risk assessment, and explainability generation.

This project is intended for educational and research purposes only and is not designed to replace professional medical diagnosis.

---

# Main Objectives

* To develop an AI-powered healthcare assistance platform
* To analyze symptoms using Natural Language Processing (NLP)
* To support image-based disease analysis
* To predict possible diseases using AI models
* To generate confidence scores and risk levels
* To provide explainable AI-based recommendations
* To maintain user diagnosis history securely
* To demonstrate multimodal AI integration in healthcare systems

* User registration and login
* Secure authentication using Firebase Authentication
* Password reset functionality

---

## 2. Symptom Input System

Users can enter symptoms in natural language.

### Example:

> “I have fever, sore throat, and headache.”

The AI system extracts important medical symptoms automatically.

---

## 3. Medical Image Upload

Users can optionally upload images related to physical or visible medical conditions.

### Supported Examples:

* Skin rashes
* Acne
* Swelling
* Eye redness
* Allergies
* Bruises
* Minor infections

Uploaded images are analyzed by an AI vision model to improve prediction accuracy.

---

## 4. AI-Based Symptom Analysis

The system uses AI models to:

* Extract symptoms
* Understand medical context
* Identify possible health conditions

---

## 5. Disease Prediction

The platform predicts possible diseases based on:

* User symptoms
* Uploaded medical images
* Dataset matching
* AI reasoning

### Example Output:

| --------------- | ---------- |

| Common Cold     | 71%        |
| Viral Infection | 63%        |

* Emergency

The AI generates recommendations such as:

* Rest and hydration
